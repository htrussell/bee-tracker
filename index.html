    Bee Health Expertise, Within Reach

    Tracker is a global AI-assisted bee health platform that helps beekeepers find the right verified specialist, wherever that expertise lives.

    Our purpose

    Niche hive problems can be difficult to diagnose locally. Tracker turns photos, videos, inspection notes, audio, sensor data, and documents into a structured case that can reach credible apiculture experts quickly and affordably.

    For beekeepers

    Compare verified specialists by expertise, price, availability, language, and regional knowledge. Submit a case in your own language and receive an expert response without being limited by local availability.

    For experts

    Receive relevant, organized cases matched to your specialty. Tracker reduces intake work while helping veterinarians, researchers, apiarists, and diagnostic professionals reach new clients globally.

    What makes Tracker different

    One platform combines multimodal evidence processing, AI-assisted triage, translation, intelligent matching, credential verification, reputation signals, secure payment, and longitudinal hive records.
